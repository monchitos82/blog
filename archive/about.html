About Me

Hello, this is Ramon!

This is my blog, I have no favorite topic to cover in it, so as my day goes, I try to add entries and frequently get rid of whatever I wrote trying to vent. Most of my entries of course are opinionated. I am an enthusiast of technology and formerly I worked as systems engineer and reliability engineer. I invest time now in doing experiments while learning emerging technologies and overall just trying to stay up to date and doing the best use of the things that draw my attention.

I had a great opportunity to educate myself in different countries and eventually to contribute to a large scale internet company. Due to some personal struggles, I had to relocate back to my country of origin (Mexico) and since then, I have written this blog across different platforms.

I like learning about new things, languages, and people, although I am not a very social person.

Thanks for your visit and feel confident to reach out via LinkedIn or by opening an issue on my GitHub profile.
