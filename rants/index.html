Rants

It is upsetting to see that decency is gone. It used to be that saving face was the ultimate form of dignity. Not anymore. Masks off, knives out. There is no shame, there are no sides. There is only pillaging and getting away with it. Some people would justify it, but there is no justification. Once you run out of folks to kick down, you are next.

I hate this place where I returned, not because everything is broken, or because incompetence is the rule and not the exception. I don't hate this place because it smells like trash every morning, or because it is littered with shit wherever you look. I don't hate this place because it is neglected and every good thing that comes around is immediately destroyed. I don't hate this place because of the excessive noise, pollution, chaos. I hate this place because those who claim to love it deeply hate it more than I do.
