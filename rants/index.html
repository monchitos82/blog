Rants

  • I still kick myself that I panicked and fled to a worse quality of life. That back in here I let recruiters downball my experience and that I didn't fight a wrongful termination due to ignorance. I regret joining a big four with the promise of finding a mentor when the only win I had was calling out abusive people on their behavior. I find frustrating that I am spending endless hours trying to convince others that I'm still relevant when evidently I have to move on and leave all this behind. I find quite challenging that I have to make ends meet with whatever I managed to save. I am positive things are going to be fine, but the rates make this pill hard to swallow.

  • I am seriously curious on the rationale some people have to be complacent with businesses and contribute to their system, especially when it is evident such system is harmful to societies. It is like living in denial, not weighing the fact that those decisions likely will come and bite them in the ass. Almost like buying a house and bearing children in a land where you are a temporary visitor. What could possibly go wrong, right?

  • It is upsetting to see that decency is gone. It used to be that saving face was the ultimate form of dignity. Not anymore. Masks off, knives out. There is no shame, there are no sides. There is only pillaging and getting away with it. Some people would justify it, but there is no justification. Once you run out of fools to kick down, you are next.

  • I hate this place where I was born and I returned to. Not because everything is broken, or because everything that was good (including our education) was wiped out, or because incompetence (the same incompetence that killed my own father) is the rule and not the exception. I don't hate this place because it smells like trash every morning, or because it is littered with shit wherever you look. I don't hate this place because it is neglected and every good thing that comes around is immediately destroyed. I don't hate this place because of the excessive noise, lack of regard for others and chaos. I hate this place because those who claim to love it, deeply hate it more than I do. But the most, I hate this place because I'm trapped in here. I cannot leave and definitely I cannot fix this place, it doesn't want to be fixed.
