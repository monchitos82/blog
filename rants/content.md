# Rants
---

* The more time I spend with LLMs the more I am convinced these should not be deployed everywhere. Leaving aside the dynamics of destroying livelihood to create a vacuum justifying their usage, LLMs are great tools that should be treated as assistants rather than replacements. And I believe this applies to all kinds of AI.

Let’s go back a few steps and figure out what a good technology is. I think a good technology in its simplest definition is one that improves our life. Vaccines, washing machines, water filters, are some examples of that idea. LLMs are good technologies too. They can help us be more knowledgeable and more productive. They can spot flaws and gaps and basically apply knowledge on the domain we use them, but they are far from perfect. And even as we keep improving them, I think we should shy away from just using them recklessly.

I want to believe the push for AI everywhere is the same as pushing screens and wifi everywhere. A sign that innovation has stalled and that pushing for relevance through looks is the ultimate strategy to milk societies disregarding the negative impact it causes on its individuals. While this is not as catastrophic as an indiscriminate use of antibiotics, I think the impact is not short of consequences.

So this rant is not about the excessive use of what’s new and flashy or the equally insatiable greed of those behind these products. My rant is on the gullibility of people that just buy an idea and push it. Just as technologists push the idea of the all knowing algorithm that knows you better than yourself, these are just exploitative techniques, not real knowledge. An obsession to create data points and use your nature to influence your decisions. This is not knowing yourself, because we are a collection of connections, not just one. No matter how much data an algorithm gets from you, it will never know you. Well, maybe these technologies would be of better use if their focus was not to measure relevance but improving your existing connections… maybe.

There is so much more I’d like to say here, but I think this is long enough already and I got hungry.
