# About Me

Hi, my name is Ramon, and this is my blog.

I used to work as a Software Engineer | Systems Engineer | Site Reliability Engineer. My formal education is computer systems and I started my career in the Central Bank of Mexico and moved through different positions and industries until I had the privilege to work for a big tech company in California, USA in 2019. In 2024 due to personal circumstances I panicked and escaped the American health system and came back to an even worse system in Mexico. I have had the opportunity to contribute to different projects and initiatives, even, to my surprise, I was awarded SRE of the year by my organization in 2023, ironically, after dismantling my own SRE team. I have proudly mentored a few colleagues who are brilliant and helped them succesfully advance their careers.

After a series of judgment mistakes, in 2025 I pulled the plug on my career and went into a pause since then.

I don't consider myself a great engineer, however, I enjoy learning, applying, and overall using technology, thus, I still write this blog from time to time.

I see **ethics, curiosity and fairness** as my compass.

When I am not doing this, I am spending time with my family, fixing things at home or being a cleaning freak. I used to play videogames, but I'm not into it anymore.
Not long ago, I tried growing my own greens, but my success is as limited as my gardening space.
