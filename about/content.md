# About me

Hi, my name is Ramon, and this is my blog.

I used to be a Software Engineer|Systems Engineer|Site Reliability Engineer. I started my career in the Central Bank of Mexico and moved through different positions and industries until I had the privilege to work for a big tech company in California, USA in 2019. In 2024 due to personal circumstances (related to type 1 diabetes) I panicked and escaped the American health system and came back to an even worse system in Mexico. I have had the opportunity to contribute to different projects and initiatives, even, to my surprise, I was awarded SRE of the year by my organization in 2023. I have proudly mentored a few colleagues who are brilliant and helped them succesfully advance their careers.

After a series of personal mistakes, in 2025 I figured out it was time to stop, so I quit whatever I was doing and went into a pause since then.

I don't consider myself a great engineer, however, I enjoy learning, applying, and overall using technology, thus, I still write this blog since I may have no job, but I still like the trade.

I see **ethics, curiosity and fairness** as my compass.

When I am not doing this, I am spending time with my family, fixing things at home or being the cleaning freak that I am.
Not long ago, I tried gardening, but I cannot grow anything else than onions, potatoes and tomatoes.
